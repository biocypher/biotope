# Airport notes

The operations desk keeps these notes on the airports we work with. Each section is one airport, under its IATA code. Last reviewed in March 2026.

## ATL

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International. The busiest airport in the world by passenger traffic for most of the past two decades.

Hub for Delta Air Lines.

## DFW

Dallas/Fort Worth International. One of the main connecting points between the coasts.

Hub for American Airlines.

## JFK

John F. Kennedy International, in Queens, New York.

Hub for Delta Air Lines, American Airlines and JetBlue.

## LAX

Los Angeles International.

Hub for American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and United Airlines.

## ORD

Chicago O'Hare International.

Hub for United Airlines and American Airlines.

## SFO

San Francisco International. United's main gateway across the Pacific.

Hub for United Airlines.

Focus city for Alaska Airlines.
